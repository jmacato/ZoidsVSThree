# Zoids VS. III translation glossary

Name priority: English anime dub > Zoids Wiki / The Iron Bible > Zoids VS. and Zoids Legacy translations > accurate reading of the Japanese. Zoids VS. III never had an English release. Zoids: Battle Legends is the US version of Zoids VS. II, and its English script is still on the VS. III disc (`etc/demo_US`), so its spellings count as official for the VS. II cast.

## Style
- Natural US English with a 2000s Zoids anime dub voice: punchy, readable, with banter for the pilots. Japanese is the source of truth; do not add or drop meaning.
- Keep each character's voice consistent.
- Plain ASCII only: straight quotes, three periods for an ellipsis, no dashes other than the hyphen.
